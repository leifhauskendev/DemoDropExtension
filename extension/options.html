Refrag

Import FACEIT demos into Refrag's 2D demo viewer. Needs a Refrag plan with the demo viewer (Prime or Team).

Checking Refrag…
DemoDrop uses your login from the Refrag website: sign in on play.refrag.gg and DemoDrop picks it up. Your password never goes through DemoDrop.
